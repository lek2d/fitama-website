    GETTING STARTED

    Meet your Fitama

    Pick an egg to start your story with Blukie, Clovie, or Ignie. Connect Apple Health and earn 100 Tama Power through your movement and workouts. Once your egg is ready, claim the hatch in the app and give your Fitama a nickname.

    Call your Blukie “Mochi” if you like. They’re still a Blukie, just with a name you picked.

    TAMA POWER

    What is Tama Power?

    Tama Power, or TP, is the energy your everyday movement and workouts bring to the adventure. Connect Apple Health and sync your activity with Fitama to earn it.

    Earn TP to work toward hatching and quest goals, then spend available TP on dungeon attacks. Your quest progress tracks eligible power you’ve earned. Your available balance shows what’s left to spend.

    Go at your own pace. You can check your current power and daily limits in the app.

    See the movement-to-TP visual guide

    TAMA POWER

    Which activities count?

    Your routine is welcome here. Walk, run, cycle, dance, or choose another workout you enjoy. Everyday movement and workouts can help you earn Tama Power when compatible activity records sync through Apple Health.

    Give your watch or workout app time to sync, then refresh your activity in Fitama. What can sync depends on your device, its Health support and the permissions you choose.

    CONNECTION

    Connect Apple Health

    1. Open Fitama on a supported iPhone and tap Connect Apple Health during setup.
    2. Review the Health permissions and allow Fitama to read the movement and workout information you want to share.
    3. Record an activity, give your device or app time to sync, then refresh activity in Fitama.

    Changed your mind about what to share? You can adjust permissions later in Apple Health. Apple’s guide to managing Health data explains how to check apps, devices, and their data sources.

    CONNECTION

    Use the wearable you already love

    Already have a wearable you like? It can join in if it, or its companion app, shares compatible movement and workout records with Apple Health. That’s how the activity reaches Fitama on your iPhone.

    1. Check your wearable app’s Apple Health sharing settings.
    2. Enable supported movement and workout sharing.
    3. Sync your activity and check that your movement or workout appears in Apple Health.
    4. Allow Fitama to read it, then refresh activity.

    Check what your device can share before you get started. Support varies by device and app. Fitama connects through Apple Health rather than directly to each wearable.

    See the movement-to-TP visual guide

    CONNECTION

    Moved around, but no TP yet?

    1. Check that your movement or workout has reached Apple Health. Not there yet? Sync your wearable or workout app first.
    2. Check that Fitama has permission to read the relevant movement and workout information in Apple Health.
    3. Head back to Fitama and refresh activity. Some devices and apps need time to sync.
    4. Check the game day and daily TP limit in Fitama. Both can affect today’s total.

    Still not looking right? Email support@fitama.app with your app version and what happened. No need to send your full health history.

    DUNGEONS & ENEMIES

    Your TP has places to be

    Your workouts and everyday movement help you earn Tama Power for the adventure. Pick an unlocked dungeon on the map, meet its level requirement, and clear earlier dungeons to move along the route.

    Choose Solo or Group, then Warm-Up (Normal) or Beast Mode (Hard). Numbered dungeon runs last 1–3 game days. Check the setup screen for the monster’s HP, daily TP limits and possible rewards before entering.

    The daily limit is two numbered dungeon entries. Active, cleared and failed runs count. A cancelled run does not use an entry if you contributed no TP; a cancelled run where you contributed TP still counts.

    Attacks spend your available TP within the run’s daily attack limit. Equipment, hearts, hunger and bond affect your companion’s strength. At zero hearts, your Fitama cannot attack or enter another dungeon until it recovers.

    The battle’s back arrow lets you leave the screen while the run continues. The separate Leave dungeon action asks you to confirm withdrawing from the run: your damage is undone and you give up its rewards. Normal returns all eligible, unexpired TP spent in that run; Hard returns 80%. Read the confirmation before leaving.

    After a win, your Fitama rests until the next daily reset. You can still earn TP through workouts and movement and complete quests. An eligible Rest Stopper can end that rest, but it does not remove the two-entry daily limit. If the dungeon timer expires, the enemy flees; checking the result later does not add another day of rest. See rewards and returning to the map for the next step.

    Training Camp has its own introductory flow and is separate from the numbered dungeon entry limit.

    QUESTS

    Daily goals. Weekly wins.

    Move. Check. Claim.

    1. Open Quest. After Training Camp, switch between Daily and Weekly to see your goals.
    2. Build progress at your pace. Refresh activity when your records have synced. Spending TP on an attack does not undo the TP you already earned toward a goal.
    3. Collect what is ready. Tap Claim and wait for confirmation. A full progress bar alone does not collect the reward.

    Daily goals reset at 4:00 AM; the weekly period starts Monday at 4:00 AM in the game’s timezone. Check the app’s current period before a late claim.

    YOUR COMPANION

    Get ready for the next adventure

    Between quests, check your Fitama’s hearts and hunger at home. Share some food when they need it, and check their status before heading into a dungeon.

    Missing a heart? A Health Potion can help. Quest rewards can help you stock up. Check each item in the app to see what it does and whether it’s available.

    TAMA POWER

    When does a new day begin?

    A new Fitama day starts at 4:00 AM in the timezone used by the game. That’s when daily goals and limits reset, rather than at midnight.

    Travelling between timezones or back from a dungeon? Check the reset and rest details in the app.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Bring a friend along

    Open Friend Search and enter your friend’s exact username or friend code. Send a request, then wait for them to accept. Incoming requests appear in Mail, where you can accept or decline.

    Your confirmed friends can see your shared profile, companion and today’s synced TP. You can remove a friendship from the friends list.

    Inviting someone new? Use Invite Friends to share your code and track their first hatch.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Invite a friend. Celebrate their first hatch.

    Open Quest → Daily → Invite Friends to share your invite link, QR code or friend code. The invite screen shows who is waiting to hatch and who has hatched already.

    1. Share your invite. Your friend can open the link or enter your friend code or username on their egg screen.
    2. Enter it before the first hatch. A new player has 72 hours from account creation to link an inviter. Only one inviter can be saved; the same Apple or Google sign-in cannot claim a new invite reward again by recreating an account.
    3. Hatch and collect. When your friend’s first egg hatches, eligible rewards arrive in Mail → Parcel. You also become friends automatically.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Check your Mail

    Mail has three tabs to help you find what needs attention:

    • Messages: updates such as NPC unlocks and achievement cards.
    • Invites: friend requests, accepted-request updates and dungeon invitations.
    • Parcel: eligible item gifts, reward boxes, NPC rewards, ranking prizes and invite rewards.

    Reading a message does not collect a parcel. Tap Claim or Receive on the reward and wait for confirmation. A sealed Mystery Package or Treasure Box goes into Inventory; open it there separately.

    If a claim is still pending, use the confirmation or recovery action on that parcel. You can pull down to sync Mail. See gifts, side-quest rewards, ranking prizes and invite rewards for more.

    Group dungeon chat is a separate conversation for the current run.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Build your dungeon party

    Choose Group when setting up an unlocked dungeon. A party can hold up to three players, including the leader. Invite confirmed friends or share the party code; incoming invitations appear in Mail.

    The leader chooses the duration and Normal or Hard difficulty before starting. Every member must meet the dungeon’s entry requirements. A pending invitation is not a joined party member, so check the roster before you enter.

    Everyone fights the same monster, but each player spends their own available TP and earns rewards based on their participation. Solo runs give lighter XP; more friends and longer runs can improve loot odds. Players can still receive different items from the same victory.

    When someone joins late or leaves an active run, the monster’s HP and rewards can change with the party. Leaving an active battle forfeits your rewards. In the lobby, a leader who leaves cancels the lobby for everyone; another member can leave while the rest of the party stays.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Talk tactics with your party

    Group dungeon chat lets party members exchange text, emoji and Fitama stickers during the same run. It is not a private DM inbox or a solo-dungeon feature.

    Open Chat from your group battle when it is available. The leader can leave a message as soon as the group battle starts, even while waiting for a friend to join. After the run ends, the conversation becomes read-only while you still have access to the results. Closing the results removes your access to that chat.

    Messages are shared with eligible teammates and processed by Fitama’s backend. Keep sensitive information out of the conversation. If someone breaks the chat rules, use Report or Block. See chat privacy and retention for the details.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Report a message. Manage your chat.

    The Chat options menu has separate Report and Block actions.

    To report, choose a teammate, select an available message, choose a reason and confirm. The report saves that message as evidence for authorized review. Reporting does not automatically block or remove the player.

    Block hides a player’s available messages from your view in current and future dungeons. They stay in the party and can still see your messages; invitations are not blocked. Use Block → Blocked players to manage your list and unblock someone.

    Keep messages respectful and avoid sharing sensitive information. For additional help, email support@fitama.app.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Follow your weekly and monthly progress

    Open Ranking and switch between Weekly and Monthly. Rankings reflect your level, earned daily Tama Power, completed quests and dungeon clears. Your username and score are visible to other signed-in players; your individual Health records are not displayed.

    The weekly period resets Monday at 4:00 AM PHT. The monthly period resets on the 1st at 4:00 AM PHT.

    Get the updates you want

    In Account Settings, enable phone notifications and choose whether to receive friend requests, dungeon invites and battle updates. Your phone’s notification permission also needs to be enabled.

    You can change those categories in Fitama or turn notifications off in your phone settings. Shared-device or lock-screen previews may show social or battle updates.

    EQUIPMENT

    Build your loadout

    Equip one weapon, one headgear and one accessory. Owning gear does not activate it: select the item in Equipment and tap Equip. Tap Magic to inspect the effects you are wearing.

    Equipment copies stay separate; consumables stack. Change gear before joining: equipment changes are blocked in both dungeon lobbies and active battles. Your attack also depends on hearts, hunger and bond, so check your companion’s status before entering.

    Browse the weapons, headgear and accessories below. Item tiers run from 1 to 3; a higher tier can have a specialized effect rather than a simple attack increase.

    TAMA POWER

    Save some power for later

    Unlock your Power Bottle with your first eligible Dungeon 1 victory. It stores spare TP for another game day. Open your owned bottle from Inventory, then use Store TP outside dungeon lobbies and battles. Newly stored TP becomes available after the next daily reset.

    Ready TP can be withdrawn for eligible quest progress and dungeon attacks, including during a battle. Pending TP stays stored. Withdrawing stored power does not create a new workout or Health record.

    Capacity grows from 200 to 400 to 600 TP. Each upgrade consumes two full bottles of stored power, with at most one upgrade charge per game day. Read the confirmation before spending the stored TP.

    ACCOUNT & PRIVACY

    Your account, your choice

    Open Account Settings and choose Delete account, then read the permanent-delete confirmation. If you use Sign in with Apple, follow the additional Apple instructions shown in the app. Logging out does not delete your account. If deletion fails, contact support@fitama.app.

    COINS & ITEMS

    What are Tama-Coins?

    Tama-Coins are the in-game currency used in the Tama Shop. They are separate from Tama Power, which fuels attacks, and XP, which advances your companion’s level.

    Sources include eligible side-quest checkpoints, reward boxes, ranking prizes, friend-invite milestone rewards and eligible Normal and Hard dungeon victories. A dungeon coin is a possible reward, not a guaranteed one. Hermitanyo’s coin ads depend on ad availability; see the current ad note.

    Claim the reward first, then check your synced balance. Spending TP does not spend coins, and owning coins does not add attack power.

    COINS & ITEMS

    Your Tama Shop field guide

    A small shopping list for a big adventure

    1. Open Tama Shop. Check your confirmed Tama-Coin balance, then browse All, Equipment or Consumables.
    2. Choose an item. Open its details to review the effect and price before buying. Use Previous and Next when there is more than one page; a page shows up to 16 listings.
    3. Confirm, then check Inventory. A purchase adds one item. Consumables stack; equipment copies stay separate. Equip purchased gear to activate it.

    If a purchase is still confirming, use its retry or recovery action before starting another purchase. The app can reject an outdated price or an insufficient balance without completing the purchase.

    DUNGEONS & ENEMIES

    What can a dungeon drop?

    Eligible victories in both Normal and Hard numbered dungeons can award items. Each eligible player has a chance to receive one Treat, Tama-Coin or XP Book from the item drop. Book tiers follow the dungeon: D1–4 Tier 1, D5–8 Tier 2, D9–12 Tier 3.

    Equipment can drop alongside that item, with a separate chance for each eligible equipment tier. Hard adds the higher-tier gear listed in the enemy atlas. Longer runs, party setup and equipped loot bonuses affect the chances. Health Potions can also drop separately from eligible victories in Dungeons 3 and 6. First-clear rewards have their own requirements.

    Participation matters. Your contribution affects XP and item eligibility. A player who contributes no damage receives no XP or loot. Eligible contributors may still receive partial XP when the enemy flees, but item drops require a victory. A possible reward in the preview is not a guaranteed drop.

    1. Review the result. Check your rewards and the party’s contributions; members can receive different items.
    2. Tap Return. This confirms any eligible reward claim and takes you back to the map. Wait for confirmation, then check Inventory and your balance.
    3. If a claim fails, use Retry. If Back to Map is offered, you can return and reopen the pending reward from the map later. Retrying does not roll a new prize.

    An older run can keep the reward rules it started with. The result shown for your run takes priority.

    Accept, sync, then choose when to finish

    1. Accept a quest on the map. Estranghero, Monkey D. Licious, Coo Coo and Kaibigang Oso share one daily bonus-quest slot with Kabutehan and Fishda. Eligible activity from earlier in that same game day can count. Finishing or cancelling does not free the day’s slot.
    2. Refresh your activity. A “waiting for sync” message is not a final zero. Allow the records for the original activity period to arrive.
    3. Review and claim. A manual Claim shows your current reward for confirmation, then grants it directly. Earned rewards settled at the deadline can arrive in Mail → Parcel; claim that package separately.

    Mumu keeps a different clock

    Mumu gives you a 24-hour sleep window from acceptance, with a 20-hour cooldown. You cannot start a second Mumu quest while one is active. Accept before sleeping and allow Fitama to read eligible Apple Health sleep records. Sleep counts toward this quest, not movement TP.

    Gear tip: wearing Watt a Workout when accepting Oso’s quest lowers the TP targets by 20%: 160–800 TP, with checkpoints at 320, 560 and 800 TP. The accepted targets stay fixed even if you change equipment later. Read the accepted card for its saved targets. A quest accepted before a rule update keeps the goals and rewards shown on that card.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Send an item to a friend

    When gifting is available, open an eligible item in Inventory, choose Gift to a friend, select a confirmed friend and review the item, quantity and optional message before sending.

    Unequip gear before gifting it. Only eligible equipment and consumables show the Gift action; coins, Power Bottles, reward boxes, Grilled Talakitok, the Ancient Compass and VitaSlash are not on the current gifting list.

    You cannot send a new gift from a dungeon lobby or active battle. The app shows your remaining send allowance. Receiving a confirmed gift remains available through Mail.

    A gift transfers the chosen item rather than making a copy. If sending is awaiting confirmation, use the recovery action before trying again. Your recipient receives the item through the gift’s Receive action.

    YOUR COMPANION

    Collect achievement cards

    Achievement cards mark milestones such as hatching your first egg, reaching companion levels, clearing solo or group dungeons, collecting equipment and defeating particular bosses.

    Open your achievement collection to inspect each card’s goal and progress. Some cards track repeated victories or collections, so completing one run may only move the counter forward.

    Achievement cards are separate from daily and weekly quest claims. An unlock does not promise a coin or item unless its reward is explicitly shown.

    ADVENTURES

    What is the Ancient Compass?

    The Ancient Compass is a permanent key awarded through an eligible, confirmed Normal Dungeon 12 victory reward claim. It belongs in Inventory and is kept as a progression item, rather than spent like a consumable.

    It points toward Map 2, which is future content in the version reviewed. Owning the compass does not mean the next map is playable yet.
