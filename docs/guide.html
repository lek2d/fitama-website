    GETTING STARTED

    Meet your adventure companion

    Pick an egg to start your story with Blukie, Clovie, or Ignie. Connect Apple Health and earn 100 Tama Power. Once your egg is ready, claim the hatch in the app and give your new buddy a nickname.

    Call your Blukie “Mochi” if you like. They’re still a Blukie, just with a name you picked.

    TAMA POWER

    What is Tama Power?

    Tama Power, or TP, is what your movement brings to the adventure. Each recorded active calorie currently becomes 2 TP, with a base daily movement limit of 1,675 TP.

    Earn TP to work toward hatching and quest goals, then spend available TP on dungeon attacks. Your quest progress tracks eligible power you’ve earned. Your available balance shows what’s left to spend.

    Go at your own pace. The daily limit is a cap, not a to-do list.

    See the movement-to-TP visual guide

    TAMA POWER

    Which activities count?

    Your routine is welcome here. Walk, run, cycle, dance, or choose another workout you enjoy. It can contribute when compatible active energy reaches Apple Health.

    The part to check is Active Energy. Fitama uses those active calories for movement TP, so your recording app or wearable needs to share them. A workout name, steps, or distance alone won’t do it.

    CONNECTION

    Connect Apple Health

    1. Open Fitama on a supported iPhone and tap Connect Apple Health during setup.
    2. Choose the activity information you’d like to share. Include Active Energy if you want to earn movement TP.
    3. Record an activity, give your device or app time to sync, then refresh activity in Fitama.

    Changed your mind about what to share? You can adjust permissions later in Apple Health. Apple’s guide to managing Health data explains how to check apps, devices, and their data sources.

    CONNECTION

    Use the wearable you already love

    Already have a wearable you like? It can join in if it, or its companion app, shares compatible Active Energy with Apple Health. That’s how the activity reaches Fitama on your iPhone.

    1. Check your wearable app’s Apple Health sharing settings.
    2. Enable Active Energy sharing if the device supports it.
    3. Sync your activity and check that Active Energy appears in Apple Health.
    4. Allow Fitama to read it, then refresh activity.

    Check what your device shares before you get started. Steps alone won’t provide the active calories needed for TP, and support varies by device and app. Fitama connects through Apple Health rather than directly to each wearable.

    See the movement-to-TP visual guide

    CONNECTION

    Moved around, but no TP yet?

    1. Look for the activity’s Active Energy in Apple Health. Not there yet? Sync your wearable or workout app first.
    2. Make sure Fitama has permission to read Active Energy in Apple Health.
    3. Head back to Fitama and refresh activity. Some devices and apps need time to sync.
    4. Check the game day and daily TP limit in Fitama. Both can affect today’s total.

    Still not looking right? Email support@fitama.app with your app version and what happened. No need to send your full health history.

    DUNGEONS & ENEMIES

    Your TP has places to be

    Pick an available dungeon on the map and meet its monster. Your TP powers your attacks, so the movement you’ve done gets a new job. Check the app for entry requirements and attack limits.

    Attacks spend available TP. Leaving a dungeon can affect rewards, restored power, and rest time; check the confirmation in your installed version before you leave. Normal and Hard modes can have different rules.

    Your companion can put their feet up while you keep earning TP and working on quests. Training Camp has its own introductory flow.

    QUESTS

    Daily goals. Weekly wins.

    Move. Check. Claim.

    1. Open Quest. After Training Camp, switch between Daily and Weekly to see your goals.
    2. Build progress at your pace. Refresh activity when your records have synced. Spending TP on an attack does not undo the TP you already earned toward a goal.
    3. Collect what is ready. Tap Claim and wait for confirmation. A full progress bar alone does not collect the reward.

    Daily goals reset at 4:00 AM; the weekly period starts Monday at 4:00 AM in the game’s timezone. Check the app’s current period before a late claim.

    YOUR COMPANION

    Get ready for the next adventure

    Between quests, check your Fitama’s hearts and hunger at home. Share some food when they need it, and check their status before heading into a dungeon.

    Missing a heart? A Health Potion can help. Quest rewards can help you stock up. Check each item in the app to see what it does and whether it’s available.

    TAMA POWER

    When does a new day begin?

    A new Fitama day starts at 4:00 AM in the timezone used by the game. That’s when daily goals and limits reset, rather than at midnight.

    Travelling between timezones or back from a dungeon? Check the reset and rest details in the app.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Bring a friend along

    Open Friend Search and enter your friend’s exact username or friend code. Send a request, then wait for them to accept. Incoming requests appear in Mail, where you can accept or decline.

    Your confirmed friends can see your shared profile, companion and today’s synced TP. You can remove a friendship from the friends list.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Check your Mail

    Mail brings together friend requests, accepted-request updates, dungeon invitations, eligible item gifts and NPC reward bundles. Open an item to see its details and the available action.

    Reading a reward message is different from receiving its contents. Use the claim or receive action and wait for confirmation. See gifts and quest rewards for more.

    Group dungeon chat is separate from Mail and is not enabled yet.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Build your dungeon party

    Choose Group when setting up an available dungeon. The party leader can invite confirmed friends, or share the party code so players can join. Incoming invitations appear in Mail.

    Party members share the monster battle and can see the roster and battle contributions. Check the lobby for entry requirements, available places and when the party can start.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Talk tactics with your party

    Not enabled yet. Group dungeon chat is being prepared for shared battles. It lets eligible party members exchange text, emoji and Fitama stickers during the same run. It is not a private DM inbox or a solo-dungeon feature.

    When available, open Chat from your group battle. After the run ends, the conversation becomes read-only while you still have access to the results. Closing the results removes your access to that chat.

    Messages are shared with eligible teammates and processed by Fitama’s backend. Keep sensitive information out of the conversation. See chat privacy and retention for the details.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Report a message. Manage your chat.

    Prepared for chat launch; not available yet. The upcoming Chat options menu includes separate Report and Block actions.

    To report, choose a teammate, select an available message, choose a reason and confirm. The report saves that message as evidence for authorized review. Reporting does not automatically block or remove the player.

    Block hides a player’s available messages from your view in current and future dungeons. They stay in the party and can still see your messages; invitations are not blocked. Use Block → Blocked players to manage your list and unblock someone.

    Chat will stay off while the safety and moderation setup is completed. For help now, email support@fitama.app.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    See how your week is going

    Your profile includes rankings so you can follow your progress alongside other players. Your username and ranking score are visible to other signed-in players; individual Health records are not shown in the ranking.

    Check the ranking period and scoring shown in your version. Ranking formats and reward availability can vary by build and enabled features; follow the rules shown in the app.

    ACCOUNT & PRIVACY

    Choose what represents you

    Edit your display name, username and profile photo from your profile. Your shared identity and companion details can appear in search, friends, parties and player cards. Your companion’s nickname is separate from their species and saved appearance.

    Height, weight, preferred activities and About Me are saved privately on your device in the version reviewed. Your activity history is separate from the social profile. Read the Privacy Policy draft for the sharing details.

    ACCOUNT & PRIVACY

    Get the updates you want

    In Account Settings, enable phone notifications and choose whether to receive friend requests, dungeon invites and battle updates. Your phone’s notification permission also needs to be enabled.

    You can change those categories in Fitama or turn notifications off in your phone settings. Shared-device or lock-screen previews may show social or battle updates.

    EQUIPMENT

    Build your loadout

    Equip one weapon, one headgear and one accessory. Owning gear does not activate it: select the item in Equipment and tap Equip. Tap Magic to inspect the effects you are wearing.

    Equipment copies stay separate; consumables stack. Change gear before joining: equipment changes are blocked in both dungeon lobbies and active battles. Your attack also depends on hearts, hunger and bond, so check your companion’s status before entering.

    Browse the weapons, headgear and accessories below. Item tiers run from 1 to 3; a higher tier can have a specialized effect rather than a simple attack increase.

    TAMA POWER

    Save some power for later

    A Power Bottle stores spare TP for another game day. Open your owned bottle from Inventory, then use Store TP outside dungeon lobbies and battles. Newly stored TP becomes available after the next daily reset.

    Ready TP can be withdrawn for eligible quest progress and dungeon attacks, including during a battle. Pending TP stays stored. Withdrawing stored power does not create a new workout or Health record.

    Capacity grows from 200 to 400 to 600 TP. Each upgrade consumes two full bottles of stored power, with at most one upgrade charge per game day. Read the confirmation before spending the stored TP.

    COINS & ITEMS

    What are Tama-Coins?

    Tama-Coins are the in-game currency used in the Tama Shop. They are separate from Tama Power, which fuels attacks, and XP, which advances your companion’s level.

    Sources include eligible side-quest checkpoints, reward boxes and eligible Normal and Hard dungeon victories. A dungeon coin is a possible reward, not a guaranteed one. Hermitanyo’s coin ads depend on ad availability; see the current ad note.

    Claim the reward first, then check your synced balance. Spending TP does not spend coins, and owning coins does not add attack power.

    COINS & ITEMS

    Your Tama Shop field guide

    A small shopping list for a big adventure

    1. Open Tama Shop. Check your confirmed Tama-Coin balance, then browse All, Equipment or Consumables.
    2. Choose an item. Open its details to review the effect and price before buying. Use Previous and Next when there is more than one page; a page shows up to 16 listings.
    3. Confirm, then check Inventory. A purchase adds one item. Consumables stack; equipment copies stay separate. Equip purchased gear to activate it.

    If a purchase is still confirming, use its retry or recovery action before starting another purchase. The app can reject an outdated price or an insufficient balance without completing the purchase.

    COINS & ITEMS

    Know what each item does

    ItemEffect and use
    TreatRestores 20 Hunger, up to 100; extra is lost. Use outside lobbies and battles.
    Health PotionNormally restores 1 heart, up to 3. Maximum 2 uses per player for the entire dungeon run; equipment can modify healing.
    Super Health PotionRestores health to 3 hearts. Use below full health and outside an active battle.
    Protein BarAdds 8% of today’s eligible earned TP, rounded down. Spending TP does not reduce the basis; previous Protein Bar boosts and restored TP are excluded. Total gain is capped at 1,675.

    The dungeon bag shows which items are usable now. “Unavailable here,” full hearts, an empty stack and a daily limit each mean different things; read the reason before trying again.

    DUNGEONS & ENEMIES

    What can a dungeon drop?

    Eligible victories in both Normal and Hard numbered dungeons can award consumables. One shared consumable roll can select a Treat, a Tama-Coin or an XP Book. The book tier follows the dungeon: D1–4 Tier 1, D5–8 Tier 2, D9–12 Tier 3.

    Equipment uses separate rolls for eligible tiers, so a victory can award more than one kind of item. Hard adds the higher-tier equipment listed in the enemy atlas. Duration, party setup, equipped loot bonuses and contribution affect eligibility or chance. A preview is not a guaranteed drop.

    First-clear progression rewards and eligible potion rewards have separate rules. An older run can retain the reward rules saved when it started.

    1. Review the results. Party members can receive different items.
    2. Finish the reward claim. Wait for confirmation, then check Inventory and your balance.
    3. Recover a pending claim. Use the app’s retry action. Refreshing the result or retrying a claim does not roll a new prize.

    Accept, sync, then choose when to finish

    1. Accept a quest on the map. Estranghero, Monkey, Coocoo and Oso each have an independent daily slot. Eligible activity from earlier in that same game day can count.
    2. Refresh your activity. A “waiting for sync” message is not a final zero. Allow the records for the original activity period to arrive.
    3. Review and claim. A manual Claim shows your current reward for confirmation, then grants it directly. Earned rewards settled at the deadline can arrive in Mail → Packages; claim that package separately.

    Mumu keeps a different clock

    Mumu gives you a 24-hour sleep window from acceptance, with a 20-hour cooldown. You cannot start a second Mumu quest while one is active. Accept before sleeping and allow Fitama to read eligible Apple Health sleep records. Sleep counts toward this quest, not movement TP.

    Gear tip: wearing Watt a Workout when accepting Oso’s quest lowers the TP targets by 20%: 160–800 TP, with checkpoints at 320, 560 and 800 TP. The accepted targets stay fixed even if you change equipment later. The card in your app takes priority for an older accepted quest.

    FRIENDS & SOCIAL

    Send an item to a friend

    When gifting is available, open an eligible item in Inventory, choose Gift to a friend, select a confirmed friend and review the item, quantity and optional message before sending.

    Unequip gear before gifting it. Only eligible equipment and consumables show the Gift action; coins, Power Bottles, reward boxes, the Ancient Compass and VitaSlash are not on the current gifting list.

    You cannot send a new gift from a dungeon lobby or active battle. The app shows your remaining send allowance. Receiving a confirmed gift remains available through Mail.

    A gift transfers the chosen item rather than making a copy. If sending is awaiting confirmation, use the recovery action before trying again. Your recipient receives the item through the gift’s Receive action.

    YOUR COMPANION

    Collect achievement cards

    Achievement cards mark milestones such as hatching your first egg, reaching companion levels, clearing solo or group dungeons, collecting equipment and defeating particular bosses.

    Open your achievement collection to inspect each card’s goal and progress. Some cards track repeated victories or collections, so completing one run may only move the counter forward.

    Achievement cards are separate from daily and weekly quest claims. An unlock does not promise a coin or item unless its reward is explicitly shown.

    ADVENTURES

    What is the Ancient Compass?

    The Ancient Compass is a permanent key awarded through an eligible, confirmed Dungeon 12 reward claim. It belongs in Inventory and is kept as a progression item, rather than spent like a consumable.

    It points toward Map 2, which is future content in the version reviewed. Owning the compass does not mean the next map is playable yet.
