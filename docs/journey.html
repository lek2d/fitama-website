Do I need a special workout?

No special routine needed. Walk, cycle, dance, or move in a way you enjoy. To count toward TP, your activity needs to record compatible active calories in Apple Health.

Can I use my wearable?

It can join the adventure if it, or its companion app, shares Active Energy with Apple Health. Fitama reads that activity on your iPhone, so compatibility depends on what your device shares. Let’s check your setup.

Moved around, but no TP yet?

Let’s check the connection. Make sure Active Energy appears in Apple Health and Fitama has permission to read it. Then sync your wearable’s app and refresh activity in Fitama. Steps alone won’t generate TP. Get a hand with syncing.

What can I do with Tama Power?

Hatch your egg, work toward quest goals, and take on dungeon monsters with your available TP. Earned quest progress and the TP you have left to spend are tracked separately. Get the TP basics.

Is Fitama coming to Android?

Yes, Android is in the plans. We don’t have a date to share just yet, but you can join the Android waitlist below.
